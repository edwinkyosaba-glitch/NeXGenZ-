Frequently Asked Questions

What is Taluscom?

Taluscom is a digital platform combining digital marketing education, remote AI opportunity discovery and international cultural connections.

Do I need previous AI experience?

Some opportunities may be suitable for beginners, while others require specific skills. Always check the eligibility requirements of each opportunity.

Does Taluscom guarantee income?

No. Taluscom should never promise guaranteed earnings. Availability, eligibility, workload and compensation vary by opportunity and provider.

How does Taluscom protect users?

Use verification, reporting, blocking, privacy controls, moderation and clear community rules to promote safer interactions.
